Group

Foundations of
Quantum Mechanics

Our group was recently established at the Institute of Theoretical Physics of São Paulo State University (IFT UNESP) in São Paulo, Brazil.

Supported by the five-year FAPESP Young Investigator Grant 2025/10927-1, “Quantum Resources and Applications in Extended Wigner’s Friend Scenarios,” our research focuses on the foundational aspects of quantum theory, particularly the Wigner’s Friend paradox and its related issues.
